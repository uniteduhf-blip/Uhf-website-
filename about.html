About us

A journey of compassion, service, and hope.

United Hands Foundation was born from a simple belief: quality healthcare and human dignity should be accessible to everyone, regardless of where they live or their financial circumstances.

Our founders

Two careers, one calling

UHF was founded by Dr. Jagruti Hankare, an Ex-Scientist with the Government of India, and Dr. Rahul, an Ex-Demographer with the Government of India. The Foundation was born from a shared vision to dedicate their knowledge and experience to serving society.

Leaving successful government careers behind, they chose a path of public service through community-based healthcare and social development.

Our mission

What we work toward

Deliver accessible, affordable healthcare to underserved communities. Promote healthy ageing through comprehensive geriatric care. Provide compassionate palliative and home-based care.

Improve the health and well-being of tribal and rural populations, and empower women, youth, and children through education, health awareness, and skill development — built on sustainable partnerships with communities, volunteers, and corporate partners.

Our journey

Key milestones since 2020

August 2020 — United Hands Foundation officially registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860, beginning with small community health initiatives.

2021–2023 — Expanded across Maharashtra through free health camps, awareness programmes, preventive screenings, and outreach services, building strong community relationships.

2024 — Strengthened impact across primary healthcare, elderly care, women and child health, and education. Honoured with the Mahatma Gandhi Vyasanmukti Seva Puraskar 2024 in recognition of our community service.

2 October 2025 — Launched Vayorang, a comprehensive geriatric day-care initiative in Panchgani dedicated to healthy ageing and dignified living for senior citizens.

2025–2026 — Regular health camps across adopted tribal villages, expanded home-based palliative care, school health initiatives, and elderly care services.

Today — More than 10,000 lives impacted, with a growing network of healthcare professionals, volunteers, donors, and partners across multiple districts of Maharashtra.

What guides us

Our values

Dignity

Every person, respected

From elderly citizens to tribal families, we design care around people's dignity and independence, never treating anyone as a case number.

Compassion

Care that reaches home

Whether it's a health camp in a remote village or palliative care at a patient's bedside, our work meets people where they are.

Equity

No one left behind

We build sustainable partnerships with communities, healthcare professionals, and corporate partners to reach those who need us most.
