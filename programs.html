Our programmes

Six ways we work alongside communities.

Real and lasting change happens when communities, healthcare professionals, volunteers, donors, and corporate partners come together with a shared purpose.

Vayorang — Geriatric Care

Healthy ageing begins with compassionate care. Our flagship geriatric programme improves the physical, emotional, and social well-being of senior citizens through comprehensive healthcare, day-care services, rehabilitation, nutrition support, mental wellness activities, and community engagement — so every elderly person lives with dignity, independence, and respect.

Palliative Care

Serious illness should never mean facing pain or loneliness alone. We provide compassionate home-based medical care, pain management, nursing support, counselling, and caregiver guidance — helping patients and families experience comfort, dignity, and an improved quality of life.

Support this

Tribal Health Outreach

Healthcare should reach every village, regardless of distance. Through regular medical camps, health screenings, free consultations, medicines, and health education, our outreach teams serve remote tribal communities — ensuring timely diagnosis, disease prevention, and continuity of care.

Support this

School Health Programme

Healthy children build a healthier future. We conduct comprehensive health check-ups, nutrition assessments, vision and dental screenings, hygiene education, mental well-being awareness, and preventive healthcare sessions.

Support this

Women & Youth Empowerment

Empowered communities begin with empowered people. We provide health awareness, menstrual hygiene education, life-skills training, leadership development, vocational training, and livelihood opportunities.

Support this

Happy Paws — Animal Welfare

Compassion extends to every living being. Happy Paws improves the welfare of stray and abandoned animals through rescue, medical treatment, vaccination, sterilisation, rehabilitation, and adoption initiatives.
