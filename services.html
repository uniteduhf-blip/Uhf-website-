Health services

Quality healthcare is a fundamental right.

Our health services are designed to reach underserved communities through preventive, curative, rehabilitative, and palliative care — ensuring compassionate, accessible healthcare for people of all ages.

Outpatient Department (OPD)

Affordable, accessible primary healthcare through qualified medical professionals — consultations, diagnosis, treatment, follow-up care, and health counselling.

General consultations · Chronic disease management · Minor illness treatment · Medication guidance · Referrals

Free Medical Camps

Regular camps in rural, tribal, and underserved communities — free consultations, essential medicines, screenings, and early disease detection.

BP & diabetes screening · Anaemia screening · Child & maternal health · Health education · Free medicines

Vayorang — Geriatric Day Care

Comprehensive medical care alongside social interaction, mental wellness, rehabilitation, and recreational activities for senior citizens.

Geriatric assessment · Physiotherapy · Nutrition guidance · Mental health counselling · Caregiver education

Home-Based Palliative Care

Compassionate medical and nursing care for people living with serious illness, delivered in the comfort of their own homes.

Pain & symptom management · Home nursing · Emotional support · Caregiver training · End-of-life care

Preventive Health & Screening

Early detection saves lives. We run regular screening programmes to catch health problems before they become severe.

Blood pressure & sugar · BMI · Vision & dental · Nutrition · Lifestyle risk assessment

School Health Programme

Preventive healthcare, early diagnosis, and health education to help school-going children build healthy habits.

Health check-ups · Vision & hearing · Nutrition · Hygiene education · Adolescent & mental wellbeing

Community Health Education

Awareness is one of the most effective tools for disease prevention — we empower communities to make informed health decisions.

Nutrition · Hygiene & sanitation · Maternal & child health · NCDs · Mental health · First aid

Emergency Medical Support

Where resources permit, we provide immediate first aid, emergency stabilisation, and referrals — connecting patients to higher-level care when it matters most.

Our commitment

Every healthcare service we provide is guided by compassion, dignity, equity, and excellence. Through community partnerships, dedicated healthcare professionals, and volunteers, United Hands Foundation continues to make quality healthcare accessible to those who need it most.
